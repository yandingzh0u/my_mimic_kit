Diagnosis
---------
The conditional representation improves local pose tracking: final root
rotation, body position, and body rotation errors are lower than the fully
trained local ADD baseline. However, the policy has a persistent forward-speed
mismatch. A small velocity bias accumulates over the 10 s evaluation window,
leaving 0.708 m root-position error. Extra training reduced this error only
from 0.842 m to 0.708 m and did not remove it. Mean episode length also fell
from 300 to 295.1 steps, indicating some non-foot-contact terminations.

The discriminator remained numerically healthy: no NaN/Inf occurred, the
final reward was 0.375, the context contribution was +0.0745, history-gradient
share was 3.80%, and the added-column weight norm was 2.37. The failure is not
an optimization collapse; it is a reward-geometry mismatch in which local
pose/context accuracy is favored over long-horizon translational speed.
