Interpretation boundary:
  The discriminator did not become a global constant. It over-separated the
  zero positive from policy negatives, then saturated the policy region at a
  near-zero softplus reward with very little variance. The shared head also
  did not make branch margins commensurate: state/motion deficits remained
  about 5.5 times the rotation deficit.

  Checkpoint ray probes found a steep unsampled shell between the zero anchor
  and policy support. The endpoint-only GP was small at zero and at policy
  negatives, while the intermediate shell had a much larger gradient. This is
  consistent with projection + LayerNorm encoding zero/nonzero as opposite
  directions on a fixed-radius latent sphere and losing a smooth radial ADD
  landscape.

  This run has half the samples of the successful v1 mean run (65.5M versus
  131.1M), so behavior alone is not a strict equal-budget proof. However, the
  checkpoint geometry, reward saturation, and failure to calibrate branches
  directly falsify the stated purpose of the shared-head design. Same-budget
  independent-head ZA runs also produced substantial winding (tau=4: 0.852;
  tau=1: 0.616), whereas this v2 run produced none.
