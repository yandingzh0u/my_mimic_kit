Interpretation:
  Clear regression versus mean fusion. The raw rotation deficit is chronically
  about half of the state/motion deficits because the rotation branch (9-dim
  input, 128 units) has an intrinsically narrower logit range than the
  state/motion branches, not because winding tracks better. The softmax
  bottleneck therefore permanently attends to state/motion and the effective
  winding weight collapses to ~0.05 (vs 1/3 under mean fusion), partially
  re-opening the non-rolling shortcut. This is the multiplicative branch-scale
  failure mode anticipated for raw (unnormalized) deficits; it motivates the
  EMA-scale-normalized deficit variant as a required v2 rather than an option.
